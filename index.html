The Kardashev Scale

Civilizations are measured by the energy they command.

Proposed by astrophysicist Nikolai Kardashev, the scale classifies civilizations by their ability to harness energy. Humanity today sits below Type I — our mission is to close that gap within 50 years.

0

Sub-Planetary

Energy drawn from raw materials — fossil fuels, early renewables. Fragmented coordination. This is humanity today.

I

Planetary

Full command of a planet's energy: fusion, global solar, weather and geo-systems. A unified, coordinated species.

II

Stellar

Harnessing the entire output of a star — Dyson-scale engineering, interplanetary industry and settlement.

III

Galactic

Commanding energy on the scale of an entire galaxy. The far horizon of intelligent life.
